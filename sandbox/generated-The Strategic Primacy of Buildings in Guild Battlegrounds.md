<h2 class="wp-block-heading"><strong>Primacy of Buildings in Guild Battlegrounds</strong></h2>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>In the complex ecosystem of Forge of Empires, Guild Battlegrounds is a multifaceted competition where victory is not determined by brute force alone. The core objective is not territorial domination but the accumulation of Victory Points per hour (VP/h) over a season. Buildings, both those constructed on the battleground map and those built within individual player cities, are the most potent and direct mechanism for manipulating this critical metric. The importance of these structures is so profound that a guild's overall performance can be predicted by its investment in its building strategy.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p><a href="https://sites.google.com/view/arrowheads-guild-forum/guild-competitions/gbg-battlegrounds">GBG - Battlegrounds - Google Sites</a></p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>This analysis deconstructs the importance of buildings into a dual-layered ecosystem. The first layer consists of the tactical, on-map structures that a guild collectively builds within owned sectors. These provide immediate benefits, such as attrition reduction and VP generation. 1 The second, more foundational layer is composed of the strategic, player-owned Great Buildings (GBs) that serve as the long-term economic and military engine for the guild's operations. 2 Without a robust portfolio of player-owned GBs, a guild cannot sustain the logistical demands required to effectively build and compete on the GBG map</p>
