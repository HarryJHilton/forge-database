# Tier 1: The Tactical Importance of On-Map GBG Buildings

The buildings erected on the GBG map are the instruments of a guild's immediate strategic goals. Their placement, type, and timing can completely alter the course of a battleground season.

#### A. The Unassailable Fortress: Guild Headquarters (HQ) Buildings

<p>The single most consequential tactical decision in GBG is the selection and construction of the building on the guild's starting province, or Headquarters (HQ). This province, which cannot be conquered by other guilds, has a single building slot that can provide a disproportionately high return on investment. The available choices offer varying degrees of attrition reduction for adjacent provinces  and significant boosts to Victory Points.

	 A specific type of HQ building, known as the Fortified Guild Fieldcamp (also referred to as the Advanced Guild Fortress or Advanced Field Garrison by the player community), stands out as a game-changer. This building not only provides a large flat bonus of up to 1000 Victory Points per hour but, more importantly, it generates a massive 100% increase to the VP from every single sector the guild owns. This mechanic creates a profound and often counterintuitive dynamic.
	 
	 The disparity in performance between guilds can be extreme; a guild with only one sector—its HQ—can collect a substantially higher VP/h than another guild that has conquered dozens of sectors but failed to build a proper HQ building. 
	 
	 This phenomenon reveals a central strategic principle: **victory is not about the quantity of territory but the quality of the buildings on that territory**. For this reason, a high-ranking guild will typically prioritize the construction of its HQ building at the very beginning of a GBG season, often using diamonds to instantly complete the time-consuming process.<span class=\"button-container hide-from-message-actions ng-star-inserted\"> 

#### B. Provincial Infrastructure: A Catalog of Tactical Advantage

<p>Beyond the HQ, each conquered province on the map can have between one and three building slots. These structures serve two primary functions:

- 1. Attrition management *and*
- 2. Victory Point generation.

Buildings like **Barracks** and **Guild Command Posts** provide a chance to not increase attrition when a player fights into an adjacent province, making large-scale advances more sustainable and less costly in terms of military units. 

**Barracks** also increase the number of advances required for an enemy to conquer a province, providing a defensive buffer.

**Defensive buildings** such as **Traps** and **Decoys** inflict double the attrition on enemy units who attempt to conquer the province, serving as an effective deterrent.

**Guild Command Posts**, in particular, offer a powerful blend of both offensive and defensive benefits. 

They provide attrition reduction for outgoing fights while also boosting the Victory Points generated by the province they are on. This dual utility makes them a highly sought-after structure for any guild focused on maximizing its VP.<span class=\"button-container hide-from-message-actions ng-star-inserted\">   </span></div></div>
The construction of these buildings requires a substantial investment from the guild treasury, with costs determined by the eras of the guild's members. This creates a direct link between a guild's tactical decisions and the logistical capabilities of its members' cities. The time it takes to construct these buildings—from a few hours to a full day—can be a major tactical hurdle, especially when racing another guild for a critical sector. In such time-sensitive scenarios, the ability to use diamonds to instantly complete construction is a decisive advantage. This dynamic underscores the importance of fostering a guild economy that can generate both a wide array of goods for the treasury and an adequate supply of diamonds from player cities.

## Tier 2: The Strategic Cornerstone of Player-Owned Great Buildings (GBs)

While the on-map buildings provide the tactical leverage in GBG, a guild's long-term success is predicated on the strategic development of its members' cities. This is primarily achieved through the careful selection and levelling of Great Buildings. 

These structures serve as the economic and military engine that powers the entire GBG operation.

### A. The Economic Engine: Powering the Guild Treasury

The most essential GBs for GBG are those that empower the guild's collective economy and individual player's ability to fight and grow.

- **The Arc:**  Widely considered the most important GB in the game for competitive guilds, the Arc generates Forge Points (FPs) and provides a significant percentage boost to all rewards from contributing to other players' GBs. This enables players to earn more FPs, blueprints, and medals from donations, which in turn can be used to acquire the goods necessary to fund guild operations. For this reason, many guilds consider an Arc to be a mandatory investment for every serious member.
- **The Observatory:** This foundational GB provides a consistent stream of guild goods, making it a valuable asset, particularly for guilds with a developing treasury.<
- **Chateau Frontenac (CF):** The CF is a key building for a highly active player. It boosts rewards from quests, which can be an excellent source of FPs, goods, and, critically, diamonds. These diamonds are invaluable for instantly rushing on-map GBG buildings when tactical timing is paramount.

### B. The Military Core: Sustaining the War Machine

Prolonged engagement in GBG requires a city capable of sustaining a large volume of combat. Certain GBs are indispensable for this purpose.

##### The Fighting Trio (Statue of Zeus, Cathedral of Aachen, Castel del Monte):

- These three GBs provide essential attack and defence bonuses for a player's army. They are relatively small and inexpensive to build, making them an ideal starting point for new and developing players.

##### Alcatraz & Arctic Orangery (AO):

- **Alcatraz** provides a daily supply of free military units, which is crucial for replenishing losses during prolonged fighting. 
- **The Arctic Orangery** is a specialized GB that significantly increases a player's attrition cap, allowing them to fight more battles without needing to wait for their attrition level to reset.

The landscape of military GBs is not static. Veteran player commentary indicates a significant shift in the competitive meta. The traditional **\"Fighting Trio\"** is now often seen as less critical for high-level players, with some even choosing to delete them as event buildings and other new content provide superior military boosts and a better return on space. 

This evolution demonstrates that a truly optimized GBG city is not a static collection of buildings but a dynamic portfolio that adapts to game updates and event releases.

### C. The Amplifiers: GBs for Strategic Resource Accumulation

Some GBs act as force multipliers, amplifying the output of a player's entire city and, by extension, their contribution to the guild.

- **Blue Galaxy (BG):** This building provides a chance to double the collection from other buildings. In a GBG context, this ability is a powerful accelerator for gathering FPs, guild goods, and other essential resources needed for battlegrounds.<
