
The Prestige system changes the way we should think about Great Building upgrades.

Previously, Great Building development was largely about deciding which existing bonus deserved the next Forge Points. Prestige adds another consideration: **which Great Buildings should be taken into Gold first, and which Gold effects are worth developing further?**

This guide gives an initial strategic priority for the 21 Great Buildings that currently have a Gold Tier.

> [!NOTE] This is a strategy guide, not an official InnoGames ranking.
> 
> The priorities are based on the published Gold effects and their likely usefulness across Guild Battlegrounds, Guild Expedition, Quantum Incursions, contributions and general city development.
> 
> Priorities may change as the new system is tested in live play.

See [[Prestige - What Changed]] for an explanation of the Prestige system itself.

---

## The Most Important Principle

**<u>Level 101 is now a strategic milestone.</u>**

For Gold-enabled Great Buildings, level 101 unlocks the new Gold effect.

This means that initially it may be better to bring several important Great Buildings into Gold rather than immediately pushing one building hundreds of levels further.

The first question is therefore:

> **Which Gold effects do I want to unlock first?**

Only after that should we decide which Great Buildings justify sustained levelling towards much higher levels.

## **First** Priority - The Core Gold Group

### The Arc

The Arc remains central to the Great Building contribution economy.

Its existing Contribution Boost applies to Copper Great Buildings. Its Gold effect provides the new **Gold Contribution Boost**, applying to contributions to Gold levels.

Players participating heavily in Great Building contribution threads are therefore likely to want a Gold Arc relatively early.

How far it should subsequently be levelled requires separate analysis.

See [[Prestige - Arc Contributions and Sniping]].

### Alcatraz

Alcatraz receives particularly important Gold combat effects for Guild Battlegrounds.

Its Gold effects include a multiplicative Attack and Defence bonus for the attacking army in GBG, together with an additional Attack and Defence boost for attacking and defending units.

For players who fight heavily in GBG, Alcatraz becomes one of the strongest candidates for early Gold development.

### Statue of Zeus

Gold Zeus introduces multiplicative Attack and Defence for the attacking army in Guild Expedition.

Zeus also retains the advantage of being a compact Great Building, making its new Gold effect particularly interesting for combat-focused cities.

For players who regularly fight through GE, Gold Zeus should be considered an early target.

### Aachen Cathedral

Aachen gains multiplicative Attack for the attacking army in Guild Battlegrounds.

Combined with the new Alcatraz effects, Aachen forms part of a particularly strong Gold GBG combination.

### Terracotta Army

Terracotta Army receives one of the broadest GE-oriented Gold combat effects.

Its Gold bonus applies multiplicative Attack and Defence to both attacking and defending armies in Guild Expedition.

That breadth makes Terracotta Army an important candidate for players concentrating on GE combat.

## Second Priority - Strong Supporting Buildings

### Himeji Castle

Himeji Castle combines two different Gold benefits:

- Enhanced Spoils of War
- Multiplicative Attack for the defending army in GBG

This combination of rewards and combat utility makes Himeji a strong secondary candidate.

### Arctic Orangery

Arctic Orangery gains Enhanced Critical Hit.

This provides a combat mechanic rather than another straightforward Attack or Defence bonus and therefore deserves close attention as Gold combat develops.

### Space Carrier

Space Carrier combines:

- increased Special Goods production
- multiplicative Attack and Defence for the attacking army in GE

That makes Space Carrier a useful hybrid of city development and combat.

### Observatory

Gold Observatory expands its guild role by producing Guild Treasury goods from adjacent eras while also adding multiplicative Defence for the defending army in GE.

For guild-focused players, this may make Observatory considerably more attractive than its position in a general ranking suggests.

## Priorities by Game Mode

The general ranking becomes less useful once a player has a clear speciality.

### Guild Battlegrounds

Initial GBG priority:

1. The Arc
2. Alcatraz
3. Aachen Cathedral
4. Himeji Castle
5. Arctic Orangery
6. Seed Vault

Alcatraz and Aachen are particularly important because their Gold effects directly enhance attacking performance in GBG.

---

### Guild Expedition

Initial GE priority:

1. The Arc
2. Statue of Zeus
3. Terracotta Army
4. Space Carrier
5. Observatory
6. Lotus Temple

Prestige gives GE several new multiplicative combat effects, so GE specialists have a substantially different upgrade path from GBG players.

---

### Quantum Incursions

Initial QI priority:

1. The Arc
2. Castel del Monte
3. The Blue Galaxy
4. The Virgo Project
5. Deal Castle

For a player heavily involved in Quantum Incursions, these buildings may deserve much higher priority than their position in the general ranking suggests.

## Economic and Specialist Gold Buildings

Not every Gold Great Building should be judged primarily by combat.

Several offer specialist abilities that may be valuable to particular cities.

### Shattered Horizon Siphon

Provides Gold Contribution Boost.

Its eventual priority will depend partly on how the new Gold contribution economy develops.

### Château Frontenac

Boosts rewards from Heroic Tavern quests.

This is potentially valuable to players concentrating on quest rewards, but it is not a universal priority.

### St. Mark's Basilica

Provides a percentage Goods Production Boost.

Its importance depends on the role conventional goods production continues to play in the player's city.

### Royal Albert Hall

Produces Goods selected from Goods buildings present in the city.

This makes it primarily an economic rather than combat choice.

### Colosseum

Enhances Military Historical Allies and can raise their effective level beyond 100.

Its value therefore depends heavily on the importance of Historical Allies to the player.

## Don't Automatically Push Everything to 500

Gold Great Buildings can continue well beyond level 200, but that does not mean every Gold GB should immediately be driven towards level 500.

Prestige introduces several competing demands:

- Forge Points
- Goods
- Coins
- Supplies
- Medals
- Copper Blueprint sets
- Gold Blueprint sets

The more important early objective may therefore be to establish a **portfolio of useful Gold effects** before concentrating resources on extreme levels.

A useful initial approach is:

**Reach Gold → establish the important effects → specialise → then push selected buildings higher.**

---

## Copper-Only Great Buildings

Copper-only Great Buildings should be treated differently.

Their effects can grow to level 200, but without a Gold Tier they currently stop there.

That does not make them poor buildings. It does mean that additional investment should be judged against the opportunity cost of developing Gold-enabled Great Buildings.

The existence of level 200 should not itself be treated as a reason to take every Copper-only building there.

---

## Blueprint Priority Matters

Gold progression introduces an important new constraint: Gold Blueprints.

The Blueprint Refinery converts a complete Copper Blueprint set into a single random Gold Blueprint for the same Great Building, and refinement is limited each day.

This makes it sensible to think about **which Great Buildings receive your blueprint resources**, rather than spreading refinement indiscriminately across every available Gold GB.

Initial candidates are likely to be the same buildings identified as strategic priorities:

- The Arc
- Alcatraz
- Statue of Zeus
- Aachen Cathedral
- Terracotta Army

The exact order should follow the player's chosen game modes.

See [[Prestige - Blueprints and Refinery]].

## Recommended Starting Strategy

For a player with several established Great Buildings approaching or already beyond level 100:

### Stage 1 - Establish the Core

Consider bringing the most useful buildings across the level 101 Gold threshold.

For a general combat-oriented player:

**The Arc → Alcatraz → Statue of Zeus → Aachen Cathedral → Terracotta Army**

### Stage 2 - Build Around Your Game

Choose the next Gold Great Buildings according to the game modes you actually play.

**GBG:** favour Alcatraz, Aachen, Himeji and supporting GBG buildings.

**GE:** favour Zeus, Terracotta Army, Space Carrier, Observatory and Lotus Temple.

**QI:** favour Castel del Monte, Blue Galaxy, Virgo Project and Deal Castle.

### Stage 3 - Deep Levelling

Only after establishing the desired Gold effects should the decision move towards which buildings justify very high levels.

That decision will depend on:

- how quickly individual Gold effects scale
- blueprint availability
- resource cost
- contribution economics
- the player's principal game modes

---

## Current Recommendation

Prestige does not appear to create one universal upgrade path.

Instead, it encourages players to build a **Gold Great Building portfolio around the parts of Forge of Empires they actually play**.

The important early decision is therefore not:

> **Which Great Building should I take to level 500 first?**

It is:

> **Which Gold effects do I need to unlock first?**

That distinction should guide our early Prestige strategy while the new system settles into live play.
