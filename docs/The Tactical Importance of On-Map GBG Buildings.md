
The buildings erected on the GBG map are the instruments of a guild's immediate strategic goals. Their placement, type, and timing can completely alter the course of a battleground season.

#### A. The Unassailable Fortress: Guild Headquarters (HQ) Buildings

The single most consequential tactical decision in GBG is the selection and construction of the building on the guild's starting province, or Headquarters (HQ). This province, which cannot be conquered by other guilds, has a single building slot that can provide a disproportionately high return on investment. The available choices offer varying degrees of attrition reduction for adjacent provinces  and significant boosts to Victory Points.  

[Q. What are the Provincial Buildings and which benefits do they bring?](https://support.innogames.com/kb/ForgeOfEmpires/en_DK/1227)

A specific type of HQ building, known as the Fortified Guild Fieldcamp (also referred to as the Advanced Guild Fortress or Advanced Field Garrison by the player community), stands out as a game-changer. This building not only provides a large flat bonus of up to 1000 Victory Points per hour but, more importantly, it generates a massive 100% increase to the VP from every single sector the guild owns. This mechanic creates a profound and often counterintuitive dynamic. The disparity in performance between guilds can be extreme; a guild with only one sector—its HQ—can collect a substantially higher VP/h than another guild that has conquered dozens of sectors but failed to build a proper HQ building. This phenomenon reveals a central strategic principle: victory is not about the quantity of territory but the quality of the buildings on that territory. For this reason, a high-ranking guild will typically prioritize the construction of its HQ building at the very beginning of a GBG season, often using diamonds to instantly complete the time-consuming process.   

#### B. Provincial Infrastructure: A Catalog of Tactical Advantage

Beyond the HQ, each conquered province on the map can have between one and three building slots. These structures serve two primary functions:

----1>attrition management and

----2>Victory Point generation. 

Buildings like **Barracks** and **Guild Command Posts** provide a chance to not increase attrition when a player fights into an adjacent province, making large-scale advances more sustainable and less costly in terms of military units. Barracks also increase the number of advances required for an enemy to conquer a province, providing a defensive buffer.

Defensive buildings such as Traps and Decoys inflict double the attrition on enemy units who attempt to conquer the province, serving as an effective deterrent.   

**Guild Command Posts**, in particular, offer a powerful blend of both offensive and defensive benefits. They provide attrition reduction for outgoing fights while also boosting the Victory Points generated by the province they are on. This dual utility makes them a highly sought-after structure for any guild focused on maximizing its VP.   

The construction of these buildings requires a substantial investment from the guild treasury, with costs determined by the eras of the guild's members. This creates a direct link between a guild's tactical decisions and the logistical capabilities of its members' cities. The time it takes to construct these buildings—from a few hours to a full day—can be a major tactical hurdle, especially when racing another guild for a critical sector. In such time-sensitive scenarios, the ability to use diamonds to instantly complete construction is a decisive advantage. This dynamic underscores the importance of fostering a guild economy that can generate both a wide array of goods for the treasury and an adequate supply of diamonds from player cities.
