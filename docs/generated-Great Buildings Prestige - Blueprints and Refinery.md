<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Prestige introduces a second tier of Great Building blueprints and, with it, a new resource-management decision.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Existing Great Building blueprints are now <strong>Copper Blueprints</strong>.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Great Buildings that can enter the Gold Tier also have <strong>Gold Blueprints</strong>, which are required as part of their higher-level progression.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>The important change is that spare Copper Blueprints are no longer simply surplus. They can now be used to help generate Gold Blueprints through the new Blueprint Refinery.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:separator -->
<hr class="wp-block-separator has-alpha-channel-opacity" />
<!-- /wp:separator -->

<!-- wp:heading -->
<h2 class="wp-block-heading">Copper Blueprints</h2>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Copper Blueprints are the blueprints we already had before Prestige.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>They continue to be available through the familiar sources, including:</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:list -->
<ul class="wp-block-list">
<li>Forge Point contributions to other Great Buildings</li>
<li>Premium purchases</li>
<li>Various reward sources</li>
<li>2:1 Blueprint trades</li>
</ul>
<!-- /wp:list -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>A complete Copper Blueprint set consists of the usual <strong>9 individual blueprints</strong> for a Great Building.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Copper Blueprints remain part of the levelling requirements for Gold Great Buildings.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:separator -->
<hr class="wp-block-separator has-alpha-channel-opacity" />
<!-- /wp:separator -->

<!-- wp:heading -->
<h2 class="wp-block-heading">Gold Blueprints</h2>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Gold-enabled Great Buildings introduce a second blueprint tier.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Gold Blueprints can be obtained from:</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:list -->
<ul class="wp-block-list">
<li>Forge Point contributions to Gold Tier Great Buildings where the contribution reward includes them</li>
<li>the Blueprint Refinery</li>
<li>2:1 trades between Gold Blueprints</li>
<li>Premium purchases</li>
</ul>
<!-- /wp:list -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Just as with Copper Blueprints, two unwanted Gold Blueprints can be traded 2:1 for a new random Gold Blueprint.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Gold Blueprints are specific to the Great Building concerned.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:separator -->
<hr class="wp-block-separator has-alpha-channel-opacity" />
<!-- /wp:separator -->

<!-- wp:heading -->
<h2 class="wp-block-heading">The Blueprint Refinery</h2>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>The new Blueprint Refinery provides a way to convert existing Copper Blueprint stocks into Gold Blueprints.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>The basic exchange is:</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p><strong>1 complete Copper Blueprint set → 1 random Gold Blueprint</strong></p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>The Gold Blueprint generated is for the <strong>same Great Building</strong> as the Copper set being refined.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>This distinction is important.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>A complete set of 9 Copper Blueprints does <strong>not</strong> produce a complete Gold Blueprint set.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>It produces <strong><span style="text-decoration: underline;">one individual random Gold Blueprint</span></strong>.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:separator -->
<hr class="wp-block-separator has-alpha-channel-opacity" />
<!-- /wp:separator -->

<!-- wp:heading -->
<h2 class="wp-block-heading">Daily Refinery Limit</h2>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Blueprint Refinement is limited to:</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p><strong>5 refinements per 24 hours</strong></p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>The cost increases with each refinement made during that period.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:heading -->
<h2 class="wp-block-heading">Using the Refinery</h2>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>The Refinery is accessed from the Great Building level-up window alongside the existing Blueprint trade facility.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Great Buildings with available Gold Blueprints can be selected within the Refinery.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>A Great Building is available for selection when at least one Blueprint for that Great Building is already available.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>This allows Blueprint management for eligible Great Buildings without repeatedly leaving the Refinery.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:separator -->
<hr class="wp-block-separator has-alpha-channel-opacity" />
<!-- /wp:separator -->

<!-- wp:heading -->
<h2 class="wp-block-heading">Why Copper Blueprints Matter Again</h2>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Before Prestige, players with established Great Buildings could accumulate very large stocks of spare blueprints with relatively little use for them.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Prestige changes that.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Every Gold Blueprint created through the Refinery consumes an entire Copper Blueprint set.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>That gives existing Copper Blueprint reserves a new role in Gold progression.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:quote -->
<blockquote class="wp-block-quote">
<p><strong>Do not assume a large stock of Copper Blueprints is now disposable.</strong></p>
<p>Prestige gives those Blueprint stocks a new use.</p>
</blockquote>
<!-- /wp:quote -->

<!-- wp:separator -->
<hr class="wp-block-separator has-alpha-channel-opacity" />
<!-- /wp:separator -->

<!-- wp:heading -->
<h2 class="wp-block-heading">Refinery Strategy</h2>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>The five-refinement daily limit means Blueprint conversion should be treated as a limited resource.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>There are two basic approaches.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:heading {"level":3} -->
<h3 class="wp-block-heading">Spread Refinement Across Several Great Buildings</h3>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>This gradually develops Gold Blueprint stocks for several buildings.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>It may suit players who want a broad selection of Gold Great Buildings progressing together.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:heading {"level":3} -->
<h3 class="wp-block-heading">Concentrate Refinement</h3>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>This directs refinery capacity towards the Great Buildings selected as immediate Gold priorities.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>For a combat-oriented player, early candidates might include:</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:list -->
<ul class="wp-block-list">
<li>The Arc</li>
<li>Alcatraz</li>
<li>Statue of Zeus</li>
<li>Aachen Cathedral</li>
<li>Terracotta Army</li>
</ul>
<!-- /wp:list -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>The exact choice should depend on the player's preferred game modes and existing Great Building levels.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:heading -->
<h2 class="wp-block-heading">Don't Forget Contribution Rewards</h2>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>The Refinery is not the only source of Gold Blueprints.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Gold Blueprints are also available from contributing Forge Points to qualifying Gold Great Building levels.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>The contribution reward for the particular level should therefore be checked before investing.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Gold Great Buildings reward up to <strong>7 contribution positions</strong>, compared with the top 5 on Copper levels.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>This creates an important relationship between:</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p><strong>Gold levelling → Gold contributions → Gold Blueprint rewards → further Gold levelling</strong></p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>How profitable that cycle becomes will depend partly on the new Gold Contribution Boost.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>That requires separate analysis.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<p>See:</p>

<!-- wp:heading -->
<h2 class="wp-block-heading">Blueprint Planning</h2>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Prestige makes Blueprint management part of Great Building upgrade planning.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Before using the Refinery, consider:</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:list {"ordered":true} -->
<ol class="wp-block-list">
<li>Which Great Buildings are already close to or above level 100?</li>
<li>Which Gold effects are most useful to your style of play?</li>
<li>Which Great Buildings already have substantial Copper Blueprint reserves?</li>
<li>Which Gold Blueprints can be obtained through contribution rewards?</li>
<li>Where will the limited daily Refinery capacity provide the greatest benefit?</li>
</ol>
<!-- /wp:list -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>The object is not necessarily to manufacture Gold Blueprints for every eligible Great Building at once.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>The more useful objective may be to ensure that Blueprint availability supports the Great Buildings you actually intend to develop.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:separator -->
<hr class="wp-block-separator has-alpha-channel-opacity" />

<!-- wp:heading -->
<h2 class="wp-block-heading">Initial Recommendation</h2>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Until we have more live experience with Prestige:</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:quote -->
<blockquote class="wp-block-quote">
<p><strong>Treat Copper Blueprints as a strategic resource rather than surplus inventory.</strong></p>
</blockquote>
<!-- /wp:quote -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Avoid refining simply because the option is available.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Instead:</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:list -->
<ul class="wp-block-list">
<li>identify your Gold Great Building priorities;</li>
<li>check existing Copper and Gold Blueprint stocks;</li>
<li>use contribution rewards where practical;</li>
<li>direct Refinery capacity towards Great Buildings you actually intend to level.</li>
</ul>
<!-- /wp:list -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>This should reduce the risk of consuming large Copper Blueprint reserves on Gold Great Buildings that later prove to be low priorities.</p>
