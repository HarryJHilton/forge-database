Buildings constitute the foundational strategic and tactical framework for success in Forge of Empires Guild Battlegrounds (GBG). 

Victory is determined by the accumulation of Victory Points (VP), not by the simple volume of territory held. A guild's ability to generate and protect these points is directly tied to the strategic deployment of on-map buildings and the synergistic development of individual players' cities through Great Buildings (GBs). 

The most critical finding from this analysis is that the initial selection and construction of a Guild Headquarters (HQ) building at the start of a season can single-handedly secure a top-tier ranking, even with minimal territorial control. This primary finding demonstrates a fundamental paradox in GBG: a small, strategically focused guild can outmanoeuvre a larger, less-organized force. 

For any guild aspiring to competitive success, the immediate, and often diamond-accelerated, construction of a top-tier HQ building is a mandatory first step that redefines the entire campaign.
