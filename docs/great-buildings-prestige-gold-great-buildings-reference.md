<h1>Gold Great Building Effects</h1>

This reference lists the 21 Great Buildings that receive a Gold Tier with the initial Great Buildings Prestige release.

Gold becomes active at **level 101**.

From levels 101-200, both the existing Copper effect and the new Gold effect continue to develop. Gold Great Buildings can continue levelling beyond level 200 and currently have no maximum level.

# Gold Great Building Effects

## Statue of Zeus

**Gold Effect:** Multiplicative Attack and Defence for Attacking Army - Guild Expedition

Multiplies the existing Attack and Defence for Attacking Army bonuses in Guild Expedition and adds the result to the total.

---

## Colosseum

**Gold Effect:** Military Ally Boost

Enhances Military Historical Allies in the same way as gaining additional levels on an Ally.

The effect can raise an Ally's effective level beyond 100.

---

## Aachen Cathedral

**Gold Effect:** Multiplicative Attack for Attacking Army - Guild Battlegrounds

Multiplies the existing Attack for Attacking Army bonus in Guild Battlegrounds and adds the result to the total.

---

## St. Mark's Basilica

**Gold Effect:** Goods Production Boost

Increases the Goods produced by buildings by a percentage.

---

## Castel del Monte

**Gold Effect:** Multiplicative Attack for Attacking Army - Quantum Incursions

Multiplies the existing Attack for Attacking Army bonus in Quantum Incursions and adds the result to the total.

---

## Deal Castle

**Gold Effect:** Multiplicative Attack for Defending Army - Quantum Incursions

Multiplies the existing Attack for Defending Army bonus in Quantum Incursions and adds the result to the total.

---

## Royal Albert Hall

**Gold Effect:** Goods Reserve

Produces Goods every 24 hours.

The Goods are randomly selected from the types of Goods buildings placed in the city.

---

## Alcatraz

Alcatraz receives two Gold effects.

### Multiplicative Attack and Defence for Attacking Army - Guild Battlegrounds

Multiplies the existing Attack and Defence for Attacking Army bonuses in Guild Battlegrounds and adds the result to the total.

### Attack and Defence for Attacking and Defending Army - Guild Battlegrounds

Attacking and defending units in Guild Battlegrounds receive a percentage boost to their Attack and Defence values.

---

## Château Frontenac

**Gold Effect:** Heroic Tavern Quest Reward Boost

When rewards are received from Heroic Tavern quests, an additional percentage is added to the reward.

---

## Lotus Temple

Lotus Temple receives two Gold effects.

### Science Ally Boost

Enhances Science Historical Allies in the same way as gaining additional levels on an Ally.

The effect can raise an Ally's effective level beyond 100.

### Multiplicative Attack and Defence for Defending Army - Guild Expedition

Multiplies the existing Attack and Defence for Defending Army bonuses in Guild Expedition and adds the result to the total.

---

## The Arc

**Gold Effect:** Contribution Boost - Gold

Increases contribution rewards when contributing to Gold levels of other Gold Great Buildings.

The Arc's existing Contribution Boost applies to Copper Tier Great Buildings, while the Gold Contribution Boost applies to Gold Tier Great Buildings.

See:

[[Prestige - Arc Contributions and Sniping]]

---

## Arctic Orangery

**Gold Effect:** Enhanced Critical Hit

Provides a chance to deal 150% damage against enemy units from any Age except the Age of the player's own unit.

The effect does not apply to Rogues.

---

## Seed Vault

Seed Vault receives two Gold effects.

### Improved Helping Hands

Adds a chance of receiving a better reward when aiding other players.

### Multiplicative Attack and Defence for Defending Army - Guild Battlegrounds

Multiplies the existing Attack and Defence for Defending Army bonuses in Guild Battlegrounds and adds the result to the total.

---

## The Kraken

**Gold Effect:** Second Strike

During the first few battles, provides a chance to kill one enemy unit at the start of the second wave.

---

## The Blue Galaxy

**Gold Effect:** Quantum Incursions Double Collection

During the first few collections in the Quantum Incursions settlement, provides a chance for the collection to be doubled.

The settlement Town Hall is excluded.

---

## Terracotta Army

**Gold Effect:** Multiplicative Attack and Defence for Attacking and Defending Army - Guild Expedition

Multiplies the existing Attack and Defence bonuses for both attacking and defending armies in Guild Expedition and adds the result to the total.

---

## Himeji Castle

Himeji Castle receives two Gold effects.

### Enhanced Spoils of War

Adds a chance of receiving a better reward when winning fights in the main settlement.

### Multiplicative Attack for Defending Army - Guild Battlegrounds

Multiplies the existing Attack for Defending Army bonus in Guild Battlegrounds and adds the result to the total.

---

## The Virgo Project

The Virgo Project receives two Gold effects.

### Missile Launch - Second Wave

During the first few battles, provides a chance to kill half the enemy units at the start of the second wave.

### Multiplicative Defence for Attacking Army - Quantum Incursions

Multiplies the existing Defence for Attacking Army bonus in Quantum Incursions and adds the result to the total.

---

## Space Carrier

Space Carrier receives two Gold effects.

### Special Goods Production Boost

Increases the Special Goods produced by buildings by a percentage.

### Multiplicative Attack and Defence for Attacking Army - Guild Expedition

Multiplies the existing Attack and Defence for Attacking Army bonuses in Guild Expedition and adds the result to the total.

---

## Observatory

Observatory receives two Gold effects.

### Guild Goods - Adjacent Eras

Produces some of each Good from:

- the previous Era
- the current Era
- the next Era

The Goods are produced for the Guild Treasury and can only be collected while the player is in a Guild.

### Multiplicative Defence for Defending Army - Guild Expedition

Multiplies the existing Defence for Defending Army bonus in Guild Expedition and adds the result to the total.

---

## Shattered Horizon Siphon

**Gold Effect:** Contribution Boost - Gold

Increases contribution rewards when contributing to Gold levels of other Gold Great Buildings.

Its Copper effect is also changed under Prestige.

The previous Attack and Defence effect becomes a multiplicative Attack and Defence effect for attacking and defending armies.

# Gold Tier Progression

All 21 Gold-enabled Great Buildings share the same basic Prestige progression:

|Level|Status|
|---|---|
|1-100|Copper Tier|
|101|Gold Tier unlocks|
|101-200|Copper and Gold effects both develop|
|Above 200|Gold progression continues|
|Above 500|Levelling can continue; Ranking Points and contribution rewards continue|

Gold effects continue growing as the Great Building is levelled, although the gains become smaller as the effects approach their ceilings.

## Great Building Cost Data##

> [!NOTE] Prestige cost data requires a separate Forge database review before being incorporated here. The new cost structure affects existing Great Building data, relationships and dependent outputs.

The total costs supplied for a Gold Great Building at level 500 are broadly similar across the 21 buildings.

Each requires:

- approximately **756 million Forge Points**
- approximately **23.788 million Goods**
- approximately **8.934 billion Coins**
- approximately **8.934 billion Supplies**
- approximately **4.467 billion Medals**
- **5,200 Copper Blueprint sets**
- **2,400 Gold Blueprint sets**

Exact Forge Point totals vary slightly by Great Building.

The detailed in-game data should be treated as the definitive value where it differs from the published rounded figures.

# Gold Blueprint Requirements

Gold Tier progression requires Gold Blueprints as well as existing Copper Blueprints.

Gold Blueprints can be obtained through:

- contributing Forge Points to qualifying Gold Great Building levels
- the Blueprint Refinery
- 2:1 trades between Gold Blueprints
- premium purchases

The Blueprint Refinery converts:

**1 complete Copper Blueprint set → 1 random Gold Blueprint**

for the same Great Building.

See:

[[Prestige - Blueprints and Refinery]]

# Contribution Rewards

Gold levels differ from Copper levels in the number of rewarded contribution positions.

|Tier|Rewarded Positions|

|Copper|Top 5|
|Gold|Top 7|

Gold levels also introduce Gold Blueprint contribution rewards where applicable.
