Prestige introduces a second tier of Great Building blueprints and, with it, a new resource-management decision.

Existing Great Building blueprints are now **Copper Blueprints**.

Great Buildings that can enter the Gold Tier also have **Gold Blueprints**, which are required as part of their higher-level progression.

The important change is that spare Copper Blueprints are no longer simply surplus. They can now be used to help generate Gold Blueprints through the new Blueprint Refinery.

---
## Copper Blueprints

Copper Blueprints are the blueprints we already had before Prestige.

They continue to be available through the familiar sources, including:

- Forge Point contributions to other Great Buildings
- Premium purchases
- Various reward sources
- 2:1 Blueprint trades

A complete Copper Blueprint set consists of the usual **9 individual blueprints** for a Great Building.

Copper Blueprints remain part of the levelling requirements for Gold Great Buildings.

---

## Gold Blueprints

Gold-enabled Great Buildings introduce a second blueprint tier.

Gold Blueprints can be obtained from:

- Forge Point contributions to Gold Tier Great Buildings where the contribution reward includes them
- the Blueprint Refinery
- 2:1 trades between Gold Blueprints
- Premium purchases

Just as with Copper Blueprints, two unwanted Gold Blueprints can be traded 2:1 for a new random Gold Blueprint.

Gold Blueprints are specific to the Great Building concerned.

---
## The Blueprint Refinery

The new Blueprint Refinery provides a way to convert existing Copper Blueprint stocks into Gold Blueprints.

The basic exchange is:

**1 complete Copper Blueprint set → 1 random Gold Blueprint**

The Gold Blueprint generated is for the **same Great Building** as the Copper set being refined.

This distinction is important.

A complete set of 9 Copper Blueprints does **not** produce a complete Gold Blueprint set.

It produces **<u>one individual random Gold Blueprint</u>**.

---

## Daily Refinery Limit

Blueprint Refinement is limited to:

**5 refinements per 24 hours**

The cost increases with each refinement made during that period.

|Refinement|Each Good|Total Goods|Coins|Supplies|
|---|--:|--:|--:|--:|
|1st|20|100|6,000|6,000|
|2nd|40|200|12,000|12,000|
|3rd|80|400|24,000|24,000|
|4th|140|700|42,000|42,000|
|5th|200|1,000|60,000|60,000|
|**Daily Total**|**480**|**2,400**|**144,000**|**144,000**|

The Goods required are from the Age of the Great Building being refined.

---

## Using the Refinery

The Refinery is accessed from the Great Building level-up window alongside the existing Blueprint trade facility.

Great Buildings with available Gold Blueprints can be selected within the Refinery.

A Great Building is available for selection when at least one Blueprint for that Great Building is already available.

This allows Blueprint management for eligible Great Buildings without repeatedly leaving the Refinery.

---

## Why Copper Blueprints Matter Again

Before Prestige, players with established Great Buildings could accumulate very large stocks of spare blueprints with relatively little use for them.

Prestige changes that.

Every Gold Blueprint created through the Refinery consumes an entire Copper Blueprint set.

That gives existing Copper Blueprint reserves a new role in Gold progression.

> [!IMPORTANT] Do not assume a large stock of Copper Blueprints is now disposable.
> 
> Prestige gives those Blueprint stocks a new use.

---

## Refinery Strategy

The five-refinement daily limit means Blueprint conversion should be treated as a limited resource.

There are two basic approaches.

### Spread Refinement Across Several Great Buildings

This gradually develops Gold Blueprint stocks for several buildings.

It may suit players who want a broad selection of Gold Great Buildings progressing together.

### Concentrate Refinement

This directs refinery capacity towards the Great Buildings selected as immediate Gold priorities.

For a combat-oriented player, early candidates might include:

- The Arc
- Alcatraz
- Statue of Zeus
- Aachen Cathedral
- Terracotta Army

The exact choice should depend on the player's preferred game modes and existing Great Building levels.

## Don't Forget Contribution Rewards

The Refinery is not the only source of Gold Blueprints.

Gold Blueprints are also available from contributing Forge Points to qualifying Gold Great Building levels.

The contribution reward for the particular level should therefore be checked before investing.

Gold Great Buildings reward up to **7 contribution positions**, compared with the top 5 on Copper levels.

This creates an important relationship between:

**Gold levelling → Gold contributions → Gold Blueprint rewards → further Gold levelling**

How profitable that cycle becomes will depend partly on the new Gold Contribution Boost.

That requires separate analysis.

[[Prestige - Arc Contributions and Sniping]]

## Blueprint Planning

Prestige makes Blueprint management part of Great Building upgrade planning.

Before using the Refinery, consider:

1. Which Great Buildings are already close to or above level 100?
2. Which Gold effects are most useful to your style of play?
3. Which Great Buildings already have substantial Copper Blueprint reserves?
4. Which Gold Blueprints can be obtained through contribution rewards?
5. Where will the limited daily Refinery capacity provide the greatest benefit?

The object is not necessarily to manufacture Gold Blueprints for every eligible Great Building at once.

The more useful objective may be to ensure that Blueprint availability supports the Great Buildings you actually intend to develop.

---

## Initial Recommendation

Until we have more live experience with Prestige:

> **Treat Copper Blueprints as a strategic resource rather than surplus inventory.**

Avoid refining simply because the option is available.

Instead:

- identify your Gold Great Building priorities;
- check existing Copper and Gold Blueprint stocks;
- use contribution rewards where practical;
- direct Refinery capacity towards Great Buildings you actually intend to level.

This should reduce the risk of consuming large Copper Blueprint reserves on Gold Great Buildings that later prove to be low priorities.
