<h1>Great Buildings Prestige   Arc Contributions And Sniping</h1>

Prestige changes one of the most established parts of Forge of Empires: the Great Building contribution economy.

For years, The Arc, contribution threads and sniping have been built around a familiar relationship between Great Building rewards and the Arc Contribution Boost.

Prestige introduces a second contribution system for Gold Great Buildings.

That means we should not automatically assume that the familiar Copper contribution rules can simply be carried across to Gold levels.

> [!IMPORTANT] **Do not assume that existing 1.9, 1.92 or 2.0 contribution calculations apply unchanged to Gold Great Building levels.**
> 
> Copper and Gold contributions now use different Contribution Boosts.

---

## Two Contribution Systems

Prestige separates Great Building contributions into two systems.

### Copper Great Buildings

The Arc's existing Contribution Boost continues to work on Copper Tier Great Buildings.

These are the contribution mechanics players are already familiar with.

### Gold Great Buildings

Gold levels use the new **Gold Contribution Boost**.

The published Prestige information identifies three sources of Gold Contribution Boost:

- Gold Tier The Arc
- Gold Tier Shattered Horizon Siphon
- Stellar Age: Discovery technologies

The important distinction is:

**Copper Contribution Boost → Copper Great Building levels**

**Gold Contribution Boost → Gold Great Building levels**

These should be treated separately when calculating contribution returns.

---

## The Arc

The Arc remains central to Great Building contributions, but Prestige gives it a second contribution role.

### Copper Arc Effect

The existing Arc Contribution Boost continues to increase rewards on Copper Great Building levels.

### Gold Arc Effect

When The Arc reaches Gold, it gains:

**Contribution Boost - Gold**

This increases contribution rewards when contributing to Gold levels of other Gold Great Buildings.

The exact strategic value of additional Gold Arc levels will therefore depend on the way its Gold Contribution Boost develops.

This is one reason The Arc remains a high priority in:

[[Prestige - Gold GB Upgrade Priorities]]

---

## Gold Contribution Rewards

Prestige also changes the number of contribution positions that can receive rewards.

|Great Building Tier|Rewarded Contributor Positions|
|---|--:|
|Copper|Top 5|
|Gold|Top 7|

Gold contribution rewards may also include Gold Blueprints.

The reward shown for the individual Great Building level should be checked before contributing.

---

## Why Seven Positions Matter

The addition of sixth and seventh rewarded positions changes the shape of a Gold Great Building contribution level.

Under the traditional Copper system, contribution planning revolves around five rewarded positions.

Gold levels provide two additional potential reward positions.

That may create:

- more opportunities to participate in a Great Building level;
- additional sources of Gold Blueprints;
- different contribution patterns between guild members;
- new possibilities for organised contribution threads.

However, the existence of seven rewarded positions does **not**, by itself, tell us what contribution multiplier should be used.

The reward values and the contributor's applicable Gold Contribution Boost still matter.

---

## What Happens to 1.9 Threads?

Traditional 1.9 threads are based on the relationship between the base contribution reward and the contributor's Arc Contribution Boost.

Prestige preserves the Copper Contribution Boost for Copper levels, so existing Copper contribution activity remains conceptually separate from Gold contribution activity.

For Gold levels, the relevant value is the player's **Gold Contribution Boost**.

Therefore:

> [!WARNING] **Do not describe a Gold position as "1.9", "1.92" or "2.0" merely because that is the multiplier normally used for Copper contributions.**

The appropriate Gold contribution calculation must be based on the Gold reward and the contributor's actual Gold Contribution Boost.

Until we have analysed the live values, Gold contribution positions should be checked individually.

---

## Gold Contribution Threads

Prestige may eventually lead guilds to operate two recognisably different forms of contribution thread:

### Copper Contribution Threads

Used for Copper Great Building levels and based on the familiar Copper Contribution Boost.

### Gold Contribution Threads

Used for Gold Great Building levels and based on Gold Contribution Boost.

Whether Gold threads eventually settle around familiar multipliers or develop their own contribution conventions will depend on the live progression values.

For now, it is safer to treat Gold contributions as a distinct system.

---

## Sniping

Sniping also needs to be reconsidered.

The underlying principle remains familiar:

A player contributes Forge Points where the resulting reward makes the contribution worthwhile.

Prestige, however, changes some of the inputs used to judge that opportunity.

For Gold Great Buildings we now need to consider:

- the base contribution reward;
- the contributor's Gold Contribution Boost;
- the position being secured;
- the possibility of Gold Blueprint rewards;
- the Forge Point investment required.

The familiar Copper assumption should therefore not be applied automatically to a Gold level.

---

## Gold Blueprints Change the Calculation

Gold Blueprints introduce another consideration.

Gold Blueprints can be obtained by contributing to qualifying Gold Great Building levels.

That means a Gold contribution may have value beyond its Forge Point return.

A player actively developing a particular Gold Great Building may place additional value on a position which awards the Gold Blueprints needed for that building.

This does not make an otherwise poor contribution automatically worthwhile.

It does mean that Forge Point return is no longer necessarily the only reason for taking a contribution position.

See:

[[Prestige - Blueprints and Refinery]]

---

## The Shattered Horizon Siphon

The Shattered Horizon Siphon also receives:

**Contribution Boost - Gold**

This provides another source of Gold Contribution Boost alongside a Gold Arc.

Its contribution role therefore deserves further analysis once the live scaling of the available Gold Contribution Boosts is understood.

The Siphon's existence is another reason not to assume that Gold contribution strategy will simply reproduce the old Arc-based system.

---

## Contribution Boosts Can Combine

The published Prestige information identifies more than one source of Gold Contribution Boost.

This means Gold contribution strategy cannot be judged solely by looking at The Arc.

The player's complete Gold Contribution Boost will be relevant when assessing Gold contribution rewards.

The interaction and progression of those bonuses should be analysed using live game values before we establish a recommended guild multiplier.

---

## Initial Guild Approach

Until the Gold contribution economy has been analysed properly, a cautious guild approach would be:

1. Continue treating Copper contributions according to established Copper rules.
2. Treat Gold Great Building levels separately.
3. Check the displayed Gold contribution rewards before placing Forge Points.
4. Check the contributor's actual Gold Contribution Boost.
5. Check whether the position includes Gold Blueprints.
6. Avoid assuming that a familiar Copper multiplier is automatically appropriate.
7. Record useful live examples so the new contribution mathematics can be verified.

---

## What We Need to Analyse

Before recommending a standard Gold contribution thread, we need reliable live information for:

- Gold Contribution Boost progression on The Arc;
- Gold Contribution Boost progression on Shattered Horizon Siphon;
- Gold Contribution Boost from relevant technologies;
- Gold Great Building contribution rewards at different levels;
- Gold Blueprint rewards by contribution position;
- Forge Point requirements across Gold levels;
- the interaction between owner investment and secured contribution positions.

Only then should we decide whether the familiar 1.9/1.92/2.0 terminology remains useful for Gold contributions or whether a separate Gold convention would be clearer.

---

## Practical Rule for Now

The safest immediate rule is:

> **Copper contributions use Copper mathematics. Gold contributions require Gold mathematics.**

Do not mix the two simply because both involve contributing Forge Points to Great Buildings.

Once sufficient live data is available, this guide can be expanded with worked examples and recommendations for guild contribution threads.
