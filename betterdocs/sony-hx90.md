					<h1>HX90V - 1/2.3 Format</h1>				
		<p>The Sony HX90V Is A Compact Camera Of The Type Known As A Superzoom. These Little Cameras Have An Extended Reach, In This Case From FF Equiv Of 24-720mm. Better Still, With The Built In Stability System It Is All Useful.</p>
<p><!-- /wp:paragraph --><!-- wp:paragraph --></p>
<p>This camera is used alongside my Panasonic camera and sometimes instead of it. It produces lovely sharp images andd the telephoto reach is astounding.</p><p>The HX90V is truly excellent pocket camera that has, in addition to the above mentioned zoom range, built in GPS. It sits in my camera bag with my main camera and has been known to be used often purely for location recording.</p>
