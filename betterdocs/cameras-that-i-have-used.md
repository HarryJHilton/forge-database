<h1>Cameras That I have used</h1>

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p><strong>I have owned many cameras that </strong><strong>have contributed to the images on this site.</strong></p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:heading {"level":1} -->
<h1 class="wp-block-heading">Digital Cameras</h1>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:heading -->
<h2 class="wp-block-heading">Nikon</h2>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>I have owned three Nikon cameras, two DSLR (Digital Single Lens Reflex) cameras and a pocket camera.<br>From each end of the spectrum my two DSLR cameras were a D3100 (beginners) and a D750 (professional) with a small collection of lenses for each. They are of different formats, APS-C or DX in Nikon speak and full frame &nbsp;- FX - &nbsp;in Nikon terminology. &nbsp;<span style="font-size: inherit;">My Pocket camera is an S550 and has a tiny 1/2.3 sensor with 10.1 mPx resolution.</span></p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:heading -->
<h2 class="wp-block-heading">Sony</h2>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>I currently own a Sony compact superzoom camera - with full frame equivalent of 720mm reach.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:heading -->
<h2 class="wp-block-heading">Panasonic</h2>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>I am using a Panasonic Lumix GX9 as my main camera having used a Panasonic Lumix G80 with a selection of lenses previously. I have updated my selection of lenses for this camera. I have an Olympus 12-40mm f2.8 Pro lens and an Olympus 14-150mm lens.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:heading -->
<h2 class="wp-block-heading">Fujinon</h2>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>In my camera "museum" (junk box) I have a Fuji 6800Z. This was, and still is, an excellent little camera that has taken many of my photos. This camera was designed by Porsche so has design as well as functionality to its credits.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:heading {"level":1} -->
<h1 class="wp-block-heading">Film Cameras</h1>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>My favourite film camera was a Pentax MX Black edition that I have passed to my daughter for posterity. A superb camera and lens to learn how to take photographs with.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>I also have a 6cm (220 Roll Film) Twin Lens Reflex camera that is shall we say interesting. The images are excellent but it does take a little getting used to.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>I have a Fuji FZ-2000 Zoom camera, autofocus and electronic zoom; This was my first autofocus camera and has a useful 45-105mm zoom.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>There have also, over the years, been a collection of film cameras in many formats: Instamatic, 110 Roll film, Rangefinder and box camera. The rangefinder was a Ricoh and was an excellent camera- part exchanged for a Konica SLR that was also a good value for money model.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->
