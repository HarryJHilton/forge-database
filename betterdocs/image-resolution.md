<h1>Image  Resolution</h1>

<!-- wp:paragraph {"dropCap":true} -->
<p class="has-drop-cap">Many images on this site are available in both high-resolution and low-resolution versions. Most photographs were taken with the camera featured on the page and, in some cases, are available at very high resolutions. Low-resolution versions have been created to improve page loading times.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Gallery images are displayed in WebP format, a highly compressed image format that helps pages load more quickly. Both high-resolution and low-resolution files are available, although they are not automatically selected for display.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Low-resolution images are considerably smaller and will load faster, particularly on slower connections. However, if you are using a high-resolution display such as an Apple Retina or Microsoft Surface device, the high-resolution versions may provide noticeably better image quality where available.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>For most visitors with modern broadband connections, high-resolution images should display without any issues.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->
