<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>The Nikon D3100 sits at the opposite end of the Nikon DSLR spectrum to the D750  - in the beginners category - (by Nikon standards that is). A much simpler camera that the D750 but nevertheless an extremely good camera. I owned the D3100 from 2011 to 2016 and have many thousands of images taken with it and the selection of lenses that I acquired.</p>
