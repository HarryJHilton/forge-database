<h2 class="wp-block-heading has-large-font-size">Introduction: Building Management has changed more than one screen</h2>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>The updated Building Management System brings together several jobs that previously meant moving backwards and forwards between the city, individual buildings and Inventory.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>The most useful change is not simply the addition of a Building Management window. The new system gives you different ways to start depending on the question you are trying to answer.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>You can start with your <strong>city</strong> to see what can currently be upgraded, start with an <strong>Inventory item</strong> to find out whether it has an available use, or start with a <strong>particular building</strong> to understand its upgrade path, what you already own and what you still need.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>For players with mature cities and years of event buildings, upgrade kits, Selection Kits and fragments, this can remove a considerable amount of trial and error.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>This guide concentrates on the practical questions:</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:list -->
<ul class="wp-block-list"><!-- wp:list-item -->
<li><strong>What can I upgrade in my city?</strong></li>
<!-- /wp:list-item -->

<!-- wp:list-item -->
<li><strong>Can I use this particular kit or Inventory item?</strong></li>
<!-- /wp:list-item -->

<!-- wp:list-item -->
<li><strong>What versions of this building do I already have?</strong></li>
<!-- /wp:list-item -->

<!-- wp:list-item -->
<li><strong>What do I still need to complete its progression?</strong></li>
<!-- /wp:list-item -->

<!-- wp:list-item -->
<li><strong>Where is the building in my city?</strong></li>
<!-- /wp:list-item -->

<!-- wp:list-item -->
<li><strong>What opportunities are sitting unused in Inventory?</strong></li>
<!-- /wp:list-item --></ul>
<!-- /wp:list -->

<!-- wp:quote -->
<blockquote class="wp-block-quote"><!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p><strong>Scope note:</strong> Great Buildings are not included in the updated Building Management system covered by this guide.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph --></blockquote>
<!-- /wp:quote -->

<h2 class="wp-block-heading has-large-font-size">What are you trying to do?</h2>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>The easiest way to understand the new system is to start with the problem rather than the name of the feature.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:details -->
<details class="wp-block-details"><summary></summary><!-- wp:heading {"level":3} -->
<h3 class="wp-block-heading">I want to see everything I can currently upgrade in my city</h3>
<!-- /wp:heading --></details>
<!-- /wp:details -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p><strong>Start with: Build → Upgrade</strong></p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Upgrade changes the city into a visual selection view showing the buildings and objects that are currently available for upgrading.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:separator -->
<hr class="wp-block-separator has-alpha-channel-opacity"/>
<!-- /wp:separator -->

<!-- wp:heading {"level":3} -->
<h3 class="wp-block-heading">I have an item in Inventory and want to know whether I can use it</h3>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p><strong>Start with: Inventory → Hammer</strong></p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>The hammer gives you an immediate availability indicator.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p><strong>Grey hammer</strong><br>No action is currently available for that item.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p><strong>Gold hammer</strong><br>The item has an available action.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>A gold hammer can lead to an existing city target <strong>or</strong> to an undeployed opportunity such as a usable Selection Kit.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:separator -->
<hr class="wp-block-separator has-alpha-channel-opacity"/>
<!-- /wp:separator -->

<!-- wp:heading {"level":3} -->
<h3 class="wp-block-heading">I want to understand a particular building and its upgrade path</h3>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p><strong>Start with: Building Management</strong></p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Building Management brings together the versions you already have in the city, the complete progression, what is needed for one full building and matching items already held in Inventory.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:separator -->
<hr class="wp-block-separator has-alpha-channel-opacity"/>
<!-- /wp:separator -->

<!-- wp:heading {"level":3} -->
<h3 class="wp-block-heading">I know I own a building but cannot find it</h3>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p><strong>Start with: View</strong></p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>View takes you back to the appropriate city location and visually isolates the relevant building from its surroundings.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:separator -->
<hr class="wp-block-separator has-alpha-channel-opacity"/>
<!-- /wp:separator -->

<!-- wp:heading {"level":3} -->
<h3 class="wp-block-heading">I can do something, but I am not sure whether I should</h3>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p><strong>Stop and decide</strong></p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>The new system tells you much more clearly what is <strong>possible</strong>.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>It does not decide what is <strong>strategically sensible</strong>.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>An unused building, upgrade kit or Selection Kit may quite deliberately be:</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:list -->
<ul class="wp-block-list"><!-- wp:list-item -->
<li>kept for future use;</li>
<!-- /wp:list-item -->

<!-- wp:list-item -->
<li>waiting for another building;</li>
<!-- /wp:list-item -->

<!-- wp:list-item -->
<li>retained while you decide what to do;</li>
<!-- /wp:list-item -->

<!-- wp:list-item -->
<li>unsuitable for your current city;</li>
<!-- /wp:list-item -->

<!-- wp:list-item -->
<li>more valuable to you later than it is now.</li>
<!-- /wp:list-item --></ul>
<!-- /wp:list -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p><strong>No action is also a valid decision.</strong></p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:separator -->
<hr class="wp-block-separator has-alpha-channel-opacity"/>
<!-- /wp:separator -->
