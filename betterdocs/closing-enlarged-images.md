<h1>Closing enlarged images</h1>

<!-- wp:html -->
A lightbox will close in several way
<!-- /wp:html -->

<!-- wp:column -->
<div class="wp-block-column"><!-- wp:html -->
<ul>
<li>At the top right of each enlarged image there will normally be a cross - "X" . Some of the lightboxes used by my software require two of these crosses to be pressed.</li>
<li>Pressing the Esc key - this should always be enabled when possible.</li>
<li>Using the Back arrow key on your browser.</li>
<li>Clicking on the background, OFF the image.</li>
</ul>
