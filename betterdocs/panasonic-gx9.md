		<!-- wp:heading {"level":1} -->
 
<!-- /wp:spacer --><!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>The <strong>LUMIX GX9</strong> is a compact mirrorless camera by Panasonic, designed for creative photography. It is compact, lightweght and of the Rangefinder design form:</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph --><!-- wp:list -->
<ul><!-- wp:list-item -->
<li><strong>20.3-Megapixel Sensor</strong>: The GX9 boasts a high-resolution 20.3-megapixel sensor without a low-pass filter, ensuring excellent picture quality.</li>
<!-- /wp:list-item --><!-- wp:list-item -->
<li><strong>Tiltable Viewfinder and LCD</strong>: The tiltable electronic viewfinder and LCD screen allow you to explore unique angles and perspectives easily.</li>
<!-- /wp:list-item --><!-- wp:list-item -->
<li><strong>Dual Image Stabilization</strong>: Capture sharp images even on the move, without needing a tripod. Dual Image Stabilization ensures stability, even with long telephoto lenses.</li>
<!-- /wp:list-item --><!-- wp:list-item -->
<li><strong>In-Camera Effects</strong>: Experiment with artistic effects and filters directly in-camera. Try the new L.Monochrome and Grain Effect for timeless black and white photos.</li>
<!-- /wp:list-item --><!-- wp:list-item -->
<li><strong>4K Photo Mode</strong>: Preserve fleeting moments using the 4K Photo feature. Shoot at 30 frames per second and extract individual high-resolution frames.</li>
<!-- /wp:list-item --><!-- wp:list-item -->
<li><strong>Intuitive Design</strong>: The GX9 features an exposure dial and focus lever for seamless shooting.</li>
<!-- /wp:list-item --></ul>
<!-- /wp:list --><p>This is my current camera and is, although a different style of camera, an upgrade on the G80. I use the camera body with two Olympus lenses - a 12-40mm f:2.8 Pro lens and a 14-150mm telephoto zoom lens. The full frame equivalent range covered by these two lenses is from 24mm to 300mm - wide angle to telephoto.</p>
