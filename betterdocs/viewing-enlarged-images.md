<h1>Viewing enlarged images</h1>

Click on an image to view it in a large popup window.

The image will open is what is known as a Lightbox (a photographic term from the days when films were checked, edited and selected for use on a box with an opaque top panel and an internal electric light). I try to ensure that there is consistency in my images but I do use a variety of styles.

A lightbox will close in several ways.

At the top right there will normally be a cross - "X".Some of the lightboxes used by my software require two of these crosses to be pressed.
<ul>
 	<li>Press the Esc key - this will always be enabled when possible.</li>
 	<li>Use the Back arrow key on your browser.</li>
 	<li>Click on the background, OFF the image.</li>
</ul>
