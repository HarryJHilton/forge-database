<h2 class="wp-block-heading">Panasonic Lumix G80 - M4/3 (aka G81 and G85 in other markets)</h2>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>The Panasonic Lumix Camera range comprises mostly Of Micro 4/3 System Cameras Of Mirrorless Construction; This makes them lighter and faster in use than mirrored DSLR cameras. This was the major reason for updating (technically downgrading) from my Professional Nikon D750 Camera. The G80 (G85 In USA) is a superb M 4/3 camera and has an excellent selection of lenses available from Panasonic themselves (Leica designed); Panasonic Leica Lenses (made to more stringent Leica manufacturing standards). Leica (cost two mortgages each) and Olympus who also make excellent lenses in the M 4/3 lens mount format.</p>
