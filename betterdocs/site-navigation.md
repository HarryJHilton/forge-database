<h1>Site Navigation</h1>

<!-- wp:heading -->
<h2 class="wp-block-heading"><span class="highlight"><span class="highlight">Menu</span></span></h2>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p class="">The <span class="highlight">menu</span> on the site serves as the primary navigation tool for users to access various pages and sections. It typically includes links to important pages such as Home, About Us, Services, Contact, etc.
</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p class="">Submenus that appear when users hover over or click on a specific <span class="highlight">menu</span> item provide access to additional pages or categories related to the main <span class="highlight">menu</span> item. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:heading -->
<h2 class="wp-block-heading"><span class="highlight"><span class="highlight">Posts</span></span></h2>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p class=""><span class="highlight">Posts</span> are individual pieces of content created on the site. They are commonly used for articles, blog entries, news updates, and more. Users can browse <span class="highlight">posts</span> chronologically or based on categories and tags.
</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:heading {"level":3} -->
<h3 class="wp-block-heading"><span class="highlight">Categories and Tags</span></h3>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p class="">Categories help organize <span class="highlight">posts</span> into different topics or subjects, making it easier for users to find content of interest. Tags are more specific keywords that can be assigned to <span class="highlight">posts</span>, further enhancing searchability.
</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->
