<h1>Great Buildings Prestige   Gold GB Upgrade Priorities</h1>

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>The Prestige system changes the way we should think about Great Building upgrades.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Previously, Great Building development was largely about deciding which existing bonus deserved the next Forge Points. Prestige adds another consideration: <strong>which Great Buildings should be taken into Gold first, and which Gold effects are worth developing further?</strong></p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>This guide gives an initial strategic priority for the 21 Great Buildings that currently have a Gold Tier.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:quote -->
<blockquote class="wp-block-quote">
<p><strong>This is a strategy guide, not an official InnoGames ranking.</strong></p>
<p>The priorities are based on the published Gold effects and their likely usefulness across Guild Battlegrounds, Guild Expedition, Quantum Incursions, contributions and general city development.</p>
<p>Priorities may change as the new system is tested in live play.</p>
</blockquote>
<!-- /wp:quote -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>See Prestige - What Changed for an explanation of the Prestige system itself.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:separator -->
<hr class="wp-block-separator has-alpha-channel-opacity" />
<!-- /wp:separator -->

<!-- wp:heading -->
<h2 class="wp-block-heading">The Most Important Principle</h2>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p><strong><span style="text-decoration: underline;">Level 101 is now a strategic milestone.</span></strong></p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>For Gold-enabled Great Buildings, level 101 unlocks the new Gold effect.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>This means that initially it may be better to bring several important Great Buildings into Gold rather than immediately pushing one building hundreds of levels further.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>The first question is therefore:</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:quote -->
<blockquote class="wp-block-quote">
<p><strong>Which Gold effects do I want to unlock first?</strong></p>
</blockquote>
<!-- /wp:quote -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Only after that should we decide which Great Buildings justify sustained levelling towards much higher levels.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:heading -->
<h2 class="wp-block-heading"><strong>First</strong> Priority - The Core Gold Group</h2>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:heading {"level":3} -->
<h3 class="wp-block-heading">The Arc</h3>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>The Arc remains central to the Great Building contribution economy.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Its existing Contribution Boost applies to Copper Great Buildings. Its Gold effect provides the new <strong>Gold Contribution Boost</strong>, applying to contributions to Gold levels.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Players participating heavily in Great Building contribution threads are therefore likely to want a Gold Arc relatively early.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>How far it should subsequently be levelled requires separate analysis.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>See Prestige - Arc Contributions and Sniping.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:heading {"level":3} -->
<h3 class="wp-block-heading">Alcatraz</h3>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Alcatraz receives particularly important Gold combat effects for Guild Battlegrounds.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Its Gold effects include a multiplicative Attack and Defence bonus for the attacking army in GBG, together with an additional Attack and Defence boost for attacking and defending units.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>For players who fight heavily in GBG, Alcatraz becomes one of the strongest candidates for early Gold development.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:heading {"level":3} -->
<h3 class="wp-block-heading">Statue of Zeus</h3>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Gold Zeus introduces multiplicative Attack and Defence for the attacking army in Guild Expedition.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Zeus also retains the advantage of being a compact Great Building, making its new Gold effect particularly interesting for combat-focused cities.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>For players who regularly fight through GE, Gold Zeus should be considered an early target.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:heading {"level":3} -->
<h3 class="wp-block-heading">Aachen Cathedral</h3>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Aachen gains multiplicative Attack for the attacking army in Guild Battlegrounds.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Combined with the new Alcatraz effects, Aachen forms part of a particularly strong Gold GBG combination.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:heading {"level":3} -->
<h3 class="wp-block-heading">Terracotta Army</h3>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Terracotta Army receives one of the broadest GE-oriented Gold combat effects.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Its Gold bonus applies multiplicative Attack and Defence to both attacking and defending armies in Guild Expedition.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>That breadth makes Terracotta Army an important candidate for players concentrating on GE combat.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:separator -->
<hr class="wp-block-separator has-alpha-channel-opacity" />
<!-- /wp:separator -->

<!-- wp:heading -->
<h2 class="wp-block-heading">Second Priority - Strong Supporting Buildings</h2>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:heading {"level":3} -->
<h3 class="wp-block-heading">Himeji Castle</h3>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Himeji Castle combines two different Gold benefits:</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:list -->
<ul ist">
<li>Enhanced Spoils of War</li>
<li>Multiplicative Attack for the defending army in GBG</li>
</ul>
<!-- /wp:list -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>This combination of rewards and combat utility makes Himeji a strong secondary candidate.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:heading {"level":3} -->
<h3 class="wp-block-heading">Arctic Orangery</h3>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Arctic Orangery gains Enhanced Critical Hit.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>This provides a combat mechanic rather than another straightforward Attack or Defence bonus and therefore deserves close attention as Gold combat develops.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:heading {"level":3} -->
<h3 class="wp-block-heading">Space Carrier</h3>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Space Carrier combines:</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:list -->
<ul ist">
<li>increased Special Goods production</li>
<li>multiplicative Attack and Defence for the attacking army in GE</li>
</ul>
<!-- /wp:list -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>That makes Space Carrier a useful hybrid of city development and combat.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:heading {"level":3} -->
<h3 class="wp-block-heading">Observatory</h3>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Gold Observatory expands its guild role by producing Guild Treasury goods from adjacent eras while also adding multiplicative Defence for the defending army in GE.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>For guild-focused players, this may make Observatory considerably more attractive than its position in a general ranking suggests.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:separator -->

<h2 class="wp-block-heading">Priorities by Game Mode</h2>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>The general ranking becomes less useful once a player has a clear speciality.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:heading {"level":3} -->
<h3 class="wp-block-heading">Guild Battlegrounds</h3>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Initial GBG priority:</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:list {"ordered":true} -->
<ol ist">
<li>The Arc</li>
<li>Alcatraz</li>
<li>Aachen Cathedral</li>
<li>Himeji Castle</li>
<li>Arctic Orangery</li>
<li>Seed Vault</li>
</ol>
<!-- /wp:list -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Alcatraz and Aachen are particularly important because their Gold effects directly enhance attacking performance in GBG.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:separator -->
<hr class="wp-block-separator has-alpha-channel-opacity" />
<!-- /wp:separator -->

<!-- wp:heading {"level":3} -->
<h3 class="wp-block-heading">Guild Expedition</h3>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Initial GE priority:</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:list {"ordered":true} -->
<ol ist">
<li>The Arc</li>
<li>Statue of Zeus</li>
<li>Terracotta Army</li>
<li>Space Carrier</li>
<li>Observatory</li>
<li>Lotus Temple</li>
</ol>
<!-- /wp:list -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Prestige gives GE several new multiplicative combat effects, so GE specialists have a substantially different upgrade path from GBG players.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<hr class="wp-block-separator has-alpha-channel-opacity" />

<!-- wp:heading {"level":3} -->
<h3 class="wp-block-heading">Quantum Incursions</h3>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Initial QI priority:</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:list {"ordered":true} -->
<ol ist">
<li>The Arc</li>
<li>Castel del Monte</li>
<li>The Blue Galaxy</li>
<li>The Virgo Project</li>
<li>Deal Castle</li>
</ol>
<!-- /wp:list -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>For a player heavily involved in Quantum Incursions, these buildings may deserve much higher priority than their position in the general ranking suggests.</p>

<!-- wp:heading -->
<h2 class="wp-block-heading">Economic and Specialist Gold Buildings</h2>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Not every Gold Great Building should be judged primarily by combat.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Several offer specialist abilities that may be valuable to particular cities.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:heading {"level":3} -->
<h3 class="wp-block-heading">Shattered Horizon Siphon</h3>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Provides Gold Contribution Boost.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Its eventual priority will depend partly on how the new Gold contribution economy develops.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:heading {"level":3} -->
<h3 class="wp-block-heading">Château Frontenac</h3>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Boosts rewards from Heroic Tavern quests.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>This is potentially valuable to players concentrating on quest rewards, but it is not a universal priority.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:heading {"level":3} -->
<h3 class="wp-block-heading">St. Mark's Basilica</h3>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Provides a percentage Goods Production Boost.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Its importance depends on the role conventional goods production continues to play in the player's city.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:heading {"level":3} -->
<h3 class="wp-block-heading">Royal Albert Hall</h3>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Produces Goods selected from Goods buildings present in the city.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>This makes it primarily an economic rather than combat choice.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:heading {"level":3} -->
<h3 class="wp-block-heading">Colosseum</h3>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Enhances Military Historical Allies and can raise their effective level beyond 100.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Its value therefore depends heavily on the importance of Historical Allies to the player.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:separator -->
<hr class="wp-block-separator has-alpha-channel-opacity" />
<!-- /wp:separator -->

<!-- wp:heading -->
<h2 class="wp-block-heading">Don't Automatically Push Everything to 500</h2>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Gold Great Buildings can continue well beyond level 200, but that does not mean every Gold GB should immediately be driven towards level 500.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Prestige introduces several competing demands:</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:list -->
<ul ist">
<li>Forge Points</li>
<li>Goods</li>
<li>Coins</li>
<li>Supplies</li>
<li>Medals</li>
<li>Copper Blueprint sets</li>
<li>Gold Blueprint sets</li>
</ul>
<!-- /wp:list -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>The more important early objective may therefore be to establish a <strong>portfolio of useful Gold effects</strong> before concentrating resources on extreme levels.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>A useful initial approach is:</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p><strong>Reach Gold → establish the important effects → specialise → then push selected buildings higher.</strong></p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:separator -->
<hr class="wp-block-separator has-alpha-channel-opacity" />
<!-- /wp:separator -->

<!-- wp:heading -->
<h2 class="wp-block-heading">Copper-Only Great Buildings</h2>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Copper-only Great Buildings should be treated differently.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Their effects can grow to level 200, but without a Gold Tier they currently stop there.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>That does not make them poor buildings. It does mean that additional investment should be judged against the opportunity cost of developing Gold-enabled Great Buildings.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>The existence of level 200 should not itself be treated as a reason to take every Copper-only building there.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:separator -->
<hr class="wp-block-separator has-alpha-channel-opacity" />
<!-- /wp:separator -->

<!-- wp:heading -->
<h2 class="wp-block-heading">Blueprint Priority Matters</h2>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Gold progression introduces an important new constraint: Gold Blueprints.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>The Blueprint Refinery converts a complete Copper Blueprint set into a single random Gold Blueprint for the same Great Building, and refinement is limited each day.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>This makes it sensible to think about <strong>which Great Buildings receive your blueprint resources</strong>, rather than spreading refinement indiscriminately across every available Gold GB.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Initial candidates are likely to be the same buildings identified as strategic priorities:</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:list -->
<ul ist">
<li>The Arc</li>
<li>Alcatraz</li>
<li>Statue of Zeus</li>
<li>Aachen Cathedral</li>
<li>Terracotta Army</li>
</ul>
<!-- /wp:list -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>The exact order should follow the player's chosen game modes.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>See Prestige - Blueprints and Refinery.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:heading -->
<h2 class="wp-block-heading">Recommended Starting Strategy</h2>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>For a player with several established Great Buildings approaching or already beyond level 100:</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:heading {"level":3} -->
<h3 class="wp-block-heading">Stage 1 - Establish the Core</h3>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Consider bringing the most useful buildings across the level 101 Gold threshold.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>For a general combat-oriented player:</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p><strong>The Arc → Alcatraz → Statue of Zeus → Aachen Cathedral → Terracotta Army</strong></p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:heading {"level":3} -->
<h3 class="wp-block-heading">Stage 2 - Build Around Your Game</h3>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Choose the next Gold Great Buildings according to the game modes you actually play.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p><strong>GBG:</strong> favour Alcatraz, Aachen, Himeji and supporting GBG buildings.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p><strong>GE:</strong> favour Zeus, Terracotta Army, Space Carrier, Observatory and Lotus Temple.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p><strong>QI:</strong> favour Castel del Monte, Blue Galaxy, Virgo Project and Deal Castle.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:heading {"level":3} -->
<h3 class="wp-block-heading">Stage 3 - Deep Levelling</h3>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Only after establishing the desired Gold effects should the decision move towards which buildings justify very high levels.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>That decision will depend on:</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:list -->
<ul ist">
<li>how quickly individual Gold effects scale</li>
<li>blueprint availability</li>
<li>resource cost</li>
<li>contribution economics</li>
<li>the player's principal game modes</li>
</ul>
<!-- /wp:list -->

<!-- wp:separator -->
<hr class="wp-block-separator has-alpha-channel-opacity" />
<!-- /wp:separator -->

<!-- wp:heading -->
<h2 class="wp-block-heading">Current Recommendation</h2>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Prestige does not appear to create one universal upgrade path.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Instead, it encourages players to build a <strong>Gold Great Building portfolio around the parts of Forge of Empires they actually play</strong>.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>The important early decision is therefore not:</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:quote -->
<blockquote class="wp-block-quote">
<p><strong>Which Great Building should I take to level 500 first?</strong></p>
</blockquote>
<!-- /wp:quote -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>It is:</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:quote -->
<blockquote class="wp-block-quote">
<p><strong>Which Gold effects do I need to unlock first?</strong></p>
</blockquote>
<!-- /wp:quote -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>That distinction should guide our early Prestige strategy while the new system settles into live play.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->
