<h1>Great Buildings Prestige   Arc Contributions And Sniping</h1>

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Prestige changes one of the most established parts of Forge of Empires: the Great Building contribution economy.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>For years, The Arc, contribution threads and sniping have been built around a familiar relationship between Great Building rewards and the Arc Contribution Boost.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Prestige introduces a second contribution system for Gold Great Buildings.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>That means we should not automatically assume that the familiar Copper contribution rules can simply be carried across to Gold levels.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:quote -->
<blockquote class="wp-block-quote">
<p><strong><strong>Do not assume that existing 1.9, 1.92 or 2.0 contribution calculations apply unchanged to Gold Great Building levels.</strong></strong></p>
<p>Copper and Gold contributions now use different Contribution Boosts.</p>
</blockquote>
<!-- /wp:quote -->

<!-- wp:separator -->
<hr class="wp-block-separator has-alpha-channel-opacity" />
<!-- /wp:separator -->

<!-- wp:heading -->
<h2 class="wp-block-heading">Two Contribution Systems</h2>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Prestige separates Great Building contributions into two systems.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:heading {"level":3} -->
<h3 class="wp-block-heading">Copper Great Buildings</h3>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>The Arc's existing Contribution Boost continues to work on Copper Tier Great Buildings.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>These are the contribution mechanics players are already familiar with.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:heading {"level":3} -->
<h3 class="wp-block-heading">Gold Great Buildings</h3>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Gold levels use the new <strong>Gold Contribution Boost</strong>.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>The published Prestige information identifies three sources of Gold Contribution Boost:</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:list -->
<ul ist">
<li>Gold Tier The Arc</li>
<li>Gold Tier Shattered Horizon Siphon</li>
<li>Stellar Age: Discovery technologies</li>
</ul>
<!-- /wp:list -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>The important distinction is:</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p><strong>Copper Contribution Boost → Copper Great Building levels</strong></p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p><strong>Gold Contribution Boost → Gold Great Building levels</strong></p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>These should be treated separately when calculating contribution returns.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:separator -->
<hr class="wp-block-separator has-alpha-channel-opacity" />
<!-- /wp:separator -->

<!-- wp:heading -->
<h2 class="wp-block-heading">The Arc</h2>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>The Arc remains central to Great Building contributions, but Prestige gives it a second contribution role.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:heading {"level":3} -->
<h3 class="wp-block-heading">Copper Arc Effect</h3>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>The existing Arc Contribution Boost continues to increase rewards on Copper Great Building levels.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:heading {"level":3} -->
<h3 class="wp-block-heading">Gold Arc Effect</h3>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>When The Arc reaches Gold, it gains:</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p><strong>Contribution Boost - Gold</strong></p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>This increases contribution rewards when contributing to Gold levels of other Gold Great Buildings.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>The exact strategic value of additional Gold Arc levels will therefore depend on the way its Gold Contribution Boost develops.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>This is one reason The Arc remains a high priority in:</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Prestige - Gold GB Upgrade Priorities</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:separator -->
<hr class="wp-block-separator has-alpha-channel-opacity" />
<!-- /wp:separator -->

<!-- wp:heading -->
<h2 class="wp-block-heading">Gold Contribution Rewards</h2>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Prestige also changes the number of contribution positions that can receive rewards.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- /wp:table -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Gold contribution rewards may also include Gold Blueprints.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>The reward shown for the individual Great Building level should be checked before contributing.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:separator -->
<hr class="wp-block-separator has-alpha-channel-opacity" />
<!-- /wp:separator -->

<!-- wp:heading -->
<h2 class="wp-block-heading">Why Seven Positions Matter</h2>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>The addition of sixth and seventh rewarded positions changes the shape of a Gold Great Building contribution level.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Under the traditional Copper system, contribution planning revolves around five rewarded positions.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Gold levels provide two additional potential reward positions.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>That may create:</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:list -->
<ul ist">
<li>more opportunities to participate in a Great Building level;</li>
<li>additional sources of Gold Blueprints;</li>
<li>different contribution patterns between guild members;</li>
<li>new possibilities for organised contribution threads.</li>
</ul>
<!-- /wp:list -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>However, the existence of seven rewarded positions does <strong>not</strong>, by itself, tell us what contribution multiplier should be used.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>The reward values and the contributor's applicable Gold Contribution Boost still matter.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:separator -->
<hr class="wp-block-separator has-alpha-channel-opacity" />
<!-- /wp:separator -->

<!-- wp:heading -->
<h2 class="wp-block-heading">What Happens to 1.9 Threads?</h2>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Traditional 1.9 threads are based on the relationship between the base contribution reward and the contributor's Arc Contribution Boost.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Prestige preserves the Copper Contribution Boost for Copper levels, so existing Copper contribution activity remains conceptually separate from Gold contribution activity.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>For Gold levels, the relevant value is the player's <strong>Gold Contribution Boost</strong>.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Therefore:</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:quote -->
<blockquote class="wp-block-quote">
<p><strong><strong>Do not describe a Gold position as &quot;1.9&quot;, &quot;1.92&quot; or &quot;2.0&quot; merely because that is the multiplier normally used for Copper contributions.</strong></strong></p>
</blockquote>
<!-- /wp:quote -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>The appropriate Gold contribution calculation must be based on the Gold reward and the contributor's actual Gold Contribution Boost.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Until we have analysed the live values, Gold contribution positions should be checked individually.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:separator -->
<hr class="wp-block-separator has-alpha-channel-opacity" />
<!-- /wp:separator -->

<!-- wp:heading -->
<h2 class="wp-block-heading">Gold Contribution Threads</h2>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Prestige may eventually lead guilds to operate two recognisably different forms of contribution thread:</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:heading {"level":3} -->
<h3 class="wp-block-heading">Copper Contribution Threads</h3>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Used for Copper Great Building levels and based on the familiar Copper Contribution Boost.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:heading {"level":3} -->
<h3 class="wp-block-heading">Gold Contribution Threads</h3>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Used for Gold Great Building levels and based on Gold Contribution Boost.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Whether Gold threads eventually settle around familiar multipliers or develop their own contribution conventions will depend on the live progression values.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>For now, it is safer to treat Gold contributions as a distinct system.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:separator -->
<hr class="wp-block-separator has-alpha-channel-opacity" />
<!-- /wp:separator -->

<!-- wp:heading -->
<h2 class="wp-block-heading">Sniping</h2>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Sniping also needs to be reconsidered.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>The underlying principle remains familiar:</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>A player contributes Forge Points where the resulting reward makes the contribution worthwhile.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Prestige, however, changes some of the inputs used to judge that opportunity.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>For Gold Great Buildings we now need to consider:</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:list -->
<ul ist">
<li>the base contribution reward;</li>
<li>the contributor's Gold Contribution Boost;</li>
<li>the position being secured;</li>
<li>the possibility of Gold Blueprint rewards;</li>
<li>the Forge Point investment required.</li>
</ul>
<!-- /wp:list -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>The familiar Copper assumption should therefore not be applied automatically to a Gold level.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:separator -->
<hr class="wp-block-separator has-alpha-channel-opacity" />
<!-- /wp:separator -->

<!-- wp:heading -->
<h2 class="wp-block-heading">Gold Blueprints Change the Calculation</h2>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Gold Blueprints introduce another consideration.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Gold Blueprints can be obtained by contributing to qualifying Gold Great Building levels.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>That means a Gold contribution may have value beyond its Forge Point return.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>A player actively developing a particular Gold Great Building may place additional value on a position which awards the Gold Blueprints needed for that building.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>This does not make an otherwise poor contribution automatically worthwhile.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>It does mean that Forge Point return is no longer necessarily the only reason for taking a contribution position.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>See:</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Prestige - Blueprints and Refinery</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:separator -->
<hr class="wp-block-separator has-alpha-channel-opacity" />
<!-- /wp:separator -->

<!-- wp:heading -->
<h2 class="wp-block-heading">The Shattered Horizon Siphon</h2>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>The Shattered Horizon Siphon also receives:</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p><strong>Contribution Boost - Gold</strong></p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>This provides another source of Gold Contribution Boost alongside a Gold Arc.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Its contribution role therefore deserves further analysis once the live scaling of the available Gold Contribution Boosts is understood.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>The Siphon's existence is another reason not to assume that Gold contribution strategy will simply reproduce the old Arc-based system.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:separator -->
<hr class="wp-block-separator has-alpha-channel-opacity" />
<!-- /wp:separator -->

<!-- wp:heading -->
<h2 class="wp-block-heading">Contribution Boosts Can Combine</h2>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>The published Prestige information identifies more than one source of Gold Contribution Boost.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>This means Gold contribution strategy cannot be judged solely by looking at The Arc.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>The player's complete Gold Contribution Boost will be relevant when assessing Gold contribution rewards.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>The interaction and progression of those bonuses should be analysed using live game values before we establish a recommended guild multiplier.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:separator -->
<hr class="wp-block-separator has-alpha-channel-opacity" />
<!-- /wp:separator -->

<!-- wp:heading -->
<h2 class="wp-block-heading">Initial Guild Approach</h2>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Until the Gold contribution economy has been analysed properly, a cautious guild approach would be:</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:list {"ordered":true} -->
<ol ist">
<li>Continue treating Copper contributions according to established Copper rules.</li>
<li>Treat Gold Great Building levels separately.</li>
<li>Check the displayed Gold contribution rewards before placing Forge Points.</li>
<li>Check the contributor's actual Gold Contribution Boost.</li>
<li>Check whether the position includes Gold Blueprints.</li>
<li>Avoid assuming that a familiar Copper multiplier is automatically appropriate.</li>
<li>Record useful live examples so the new contribution mathematics can be verified.</li>
</ol>
<!-- /wp:list -->

<!-- wp:separator -->
<hr class="wp-block-separator has-alpha-channel-opacity" />
<!-- /wp:separator -->

<!-- wp:heading -->
<h2 class="wp-block-heading">What We Need to Analyse</h2>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Before recommending a standard Gold contribution thread, we need reliable live information for:</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:list -->
<ul ist">
<li>Gold Contribution Boost progression on The Arc;</li>
<li>Gold Contribution Boost progression on Shattered Horizon Siphon;</li>
<li>Gold Contribution Boost from relevant technologies;</li>
<li>Gold Great Building contribution rewards at different levels;</li>
<li>Gold Blueprint rewards by contribution position;</li>
<li>Forge Point requirements across Gold levels;</li>
<li>the interaction between owner investment and secured contribution positions.</li>
</ul>
<!-- /wp:list -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Only then should we decide whether the familiar 1.9/1.92/2.0 terminology remains useful for Gold contributions or whether a separate Gold convention would be clearer.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:separator -->
<hr class="wp-block-separator has-alpha-channel-opacity" />
<!-- /wp:separator -->

<!-- wp:heading -->
<h2 class="wp-block-heading">Practical Rule for Now</h2>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>The safest immediate rule is:</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:quote -->
<blockquote class="wp-block-quote">
<p><strong>Copper contributions use Copper mathematics. Gold contributions require Gold mathematics.</strong></p>
</blockquote>
<!-- /wp:quote -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Do not mix the two simply because both involve contributing Forge Points to Great Buildings.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Once sufficient live data is available, this guide can be expanded with worked examples and recommendations for guild contribution threads.</p>
