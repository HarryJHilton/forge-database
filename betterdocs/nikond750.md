					<h1>D750 - Full Frame (FX)
</h1>				
		<p><!-- wp:paragraph --></p>
<p>The <strong>Nikon D750 </strong>sits in the Nikon Pro Range of DSLR cameras and has a simply massive amount of features. It is quite a substantial camera with a very robust construction as is required for Pro use. This camera was my principal camera during 2015/16.</p>
<p><!-- /wp:paragraph --> <!-- wp:paragraph --></p>
<p>This was the best camera that I ever used. It's ability to take pictures in challenging situations is almost unparalleled.</p>
<p><!-- /wp:paragraph --> <!-- wp:paragraph --></p>
<p>Ill health forced me to a lighter weight camera and this particular camera was and still is sorely missed. (My current camera with a Pro grade lens attached weighs less than either the Body OR any one of my Nikon lenses)</p>
